# OrcaSlicer 0.20 mm brass nozzle — working settings log

Date: 2026-10-14  
Printer: Creality Ender-3 V3 SE, stock Creality firmware, printing through OctoPrint.  
Filament profile shown: `Creality Modified PLA @Ender-3V3-0.20 nozzle`.  
Process preset label remains `0.20 nozzle 0.12mm Fine` even when layer height is edited.

**Status:** Experimental, not a verified final calibration. Sparse infill initially failed to bond to the solid bottom; after changing nozzle temperature, layer height and infill flow, user reported that it appeared bonded. These changes were not isolated experimentally. The screenshots below reflect *different moments*, not a single verified exported preset.

## Print observations
- Earlier gold/silver multicolor PLA looked almost completely gold when printed.

- After adjustments, the copper-colored circular print's infill appeared to bond better to the solid bottom.
